# Chief Engineer Advice: visual and content protocol

Each advice is an original practical engineering guide, not a purported project news report. Publish bilingual UA/EN title, short executive summary, problem, actionable checklist, acceptance/decision criteria and a warning to follow site-specific standards and OEM instructions. Every homepage card and catalogue card links to its own complete article, using the same content/public-advice.json registry and portable relative URLs. No decorative placeholder may be presented as the finished illustration.

Each article MUST have its own topic-specific, informative, original schematic, visible on catalogue card and full article. Six initial topics: (1) CHP first start: gas train, engine/generator, cooling, protection and SCADA; (2) BESS sizing: battery, PCS, transformer, EMS and load; (3) grid connection: generator, transformer, switchgear, meter and grid; (4) efficiency: electrical and heat flows, waste heat and measurement; (5) CHP+BESS: CHP, battery, EMS, load and grid; (6) EPC tender: owner, OEM, EPC, interfaces, test/acceptance. A schematic is a conceptual editorial illustration, not an as-built drawing, certified wiring diagram or OEM-specific equipment layout. Never invent logos or present generated images as documentary photos.

Use distinct local SVG assets or equivalent original vector drawings, readable at mobile sizes; captions and meaningful bilingual alt text must identify their topic. The same visual must appear in the listing and its article. Maintain image fallback, accessible text and no hardcoded github.io links. Pre-release QA: validate every slug, image URL, image topic, image load, card→article link, article→catalogue link, UA/EN language switching and mobile display; check deployed artifact and live site separately. Do not mark deployment GREEN until confirmed.
